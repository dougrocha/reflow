Example customer page

This page stands in for a customer's website. It is served from an origin of its own, not from reflow's, and its only link to reflow is the loader script in its head: 

<script src="{{REFLOW_ORIGIN}}/embed/loader.js" data-site="demo" defer></script>

What this page proves
